About Between the Lines

A story-first approach to science, math & history.

Step inside a scene from literature — and discover the real idea hiding behind it

Between the Lines is an interdisciplinary study podcast for high-school students. Each video episode opens with one scene from a classic English or American book, explains the real science, math or history concept hidden in it, and finds the same pattern in modern pop culture or non-English literature: the modern echo.

Our method

Every episode follows the same five research steps, so each connection is checked, not guessed.

  1. 1

    Select the scene

    Choose a passage from a classic English or American text that points to something outside literature.

  2. 2

    Research the concept

    Investigate the real science, math or history behind the scene using credible sources.

  3. 3

    Check the scholarship

    Read what literary critics and the author's own writing say, so the analysis stays grounded.

  4. 4

    Find the modern echo

    Look for the same pattern in pop culture or a non-English literary tradition.

  5. 5

    Write the synthesis

    Explain how the scene, the concept and the echo all reflect one underlying pattern.

How we check each episode

We use the CIS framework (Novis-Deutsch et al., 2024): Comprehension → Integration → Synthesis.

  1. Comprehension

    Both the book and the science are explained accurately.

  2. Integration

    The connection between them is real, not random.

  3. Synthesis

    Together they reveal a new idea neither shows alone.

Meet the team

Loading the team…

Together, we started Between the Lines because we kept noticing the same thing: the best stories are usually hiding a real idea, and the best science usually has a story behind it. So we decided to stop separating the two. Our goal is to create a space to explore literature through different disciplines.
